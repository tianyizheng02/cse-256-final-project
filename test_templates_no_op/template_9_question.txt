{name}'s rate per hour for the first {x} hours she works each week is ${y}. She also receives an overtime pay of {z / 100} times her regular hourly rate, and she will also receive a ${v} bonus later this year. If {name} worked for {w} hours this week, how much are her earnings for this week?

#variables:
- name = sample(names)
- x = range(0, 100)
- y = range(0, 100)
- z = range(101, 1000)
- w = range(0, 100)
- v = range(1000, 10000)
- ans = range(0, 10000)

#conditions:
- (y * z) % 100 == 0
- w >= x
- ans = y * x + y * z / 100 * (w - x)
