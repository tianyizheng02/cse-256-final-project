{name} is planting a {fruit} tree. The last time he tried to plant a {fruit} tree, the tree died {v} {time}s after planting and he didn't earn any money from it. The tree will cost ${x} to plant. Each {time} it will grow {y} {fruit}s, which he can sell for ${z / 100} each. It costs ${w} a {time} to water and feed the tree. How many {time}s will it take before he starts earning money on the {fruit} tree?

#variables:
- name = sample(names)
- fruit = sample(["lemon", "orange", "apple", "pear", "plum", "lime", "pomegranate", "apricot"])
- time = sample(["year", "month", "week", "day"])
- x = range(0, 100)
- y = range(2, 100)
- z = range(0, 10000)
- w = range(0, 100)
- v = range(2, 100)
- ans = range(0, 10000)

#conditions:
- (y * z) % 100 == 0
- y * z / 100 > w
- x % (y * z / 100 - w) == 0
- (ans - 1) *  (y * z / 100 - w) == x
