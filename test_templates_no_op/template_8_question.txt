{name} drives for {x} hours at a speed of {y} mph and then turns around because he realizes he forgot something very important at home.  He tries to get home in {z} hours but spends the first {w} hours in standstill traffic.  He spends the next half-hour driving at a speed of {v} mph, before being able to drive the remaining time of the {z} hours going at {u} mph.  After those {z} hours have passed, he realizes that he needs to use the restroom and drives an addition {t} miles to use a rest stop.  How far is he from home at the end of those {z} hours?

#variables:
- name = sample(names)
- x = range(0, 10)
- y = range(1, 100)
- z = range(0, 10)
- w = range(0, 10)
- v = range(0, 100)
- u = range(0, 100)
- t = range(1, 100)
- ans = range(0, 10000)

#conditions:
- z > w
- z < 2 * w
- v % 2 == 0
- u % 2 == 0
- x * y >= u * (z - w - .5) + v / 2
- ans == x * y - u * (z - w - .5) + v / 2
