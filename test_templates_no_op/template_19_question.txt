{name} is hiking a {x}-mile trail. She took 1 hour to walk the first {y} miles, then another hour to walk the next {z} miles. If she wants her average speed to be {w} miles per hour, which is half of her typical running speed, what speed (in miles per hour) does she need to walk the remaining distance?

#variables:
- name = sample(names)
- x = range(2, 100)
- y = range(2, 100)
- z = range(2, 100)
- w = range(2, 100)
- ans = range(0, 100)

#conditions:
- x % w == 0
- x > 2 * w
- x > y + z
- (x - y - z) % (x / w - 2) == 0
- ans * (x / w - 2) == x - y - z
