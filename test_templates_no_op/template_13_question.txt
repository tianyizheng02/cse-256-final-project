If {name} has {y} {things} left and she plans to receive {z} more {things} later this week from her company to sell, how many did she start with?

- name = sample(names)
- things = sample(["vacuum cleaners", "refrigerators", "microwaves", "blenders", "food processors", "knives", "dishwashers"])
