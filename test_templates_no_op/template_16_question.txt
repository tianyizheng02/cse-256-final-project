Two {vehicle}s leave {city} at the same time. They begin traveling westward, both traveling for {x} {units}. The next day, they travel northwards, covering {y} {units}. What's the distance covered by each {vehicle} in the two days, if the {vehicle}s also travel back to {city} later along the same route?

#variables:
- city = sample(["San Rafael", "Seattle", "Detroit", "Kansas City", "Amarillo", "Birmingham", "Pittsburgh"])
- vehicle = sample(["train", "car", "truck"])
- units = sample(["miles", "kilometers", "yards", "meters", "feet"])
- x = range(2, 1000)
- y = range(2, 1000)
- ans = range(4, 10000)

#conditions:
- ans == x + y
