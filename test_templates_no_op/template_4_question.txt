Every day, {name} feeds each of her {bird}s {x} cups of mixed {bird} feed, containing seeds, mealworms and vegetables to help keep them healthy.  She gives the {bird}s their feed in three separate meals. In the morning, she gives her flock of {bird}s {y} cups of feed.  In the afternoon, she gives her {bird}s another {z} cups of feed.  As her {bird}s grow older, she will need to give each of them {v} times the current amount of feed.  How many cups of feed does she need to give her {bird}s in the final meal of the day if the size of {name}'s flock is {w} {bird}s?

- v = range(2, 10)
