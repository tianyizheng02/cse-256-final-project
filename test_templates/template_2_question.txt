{name} decides to try flipping a house.  He buys a house for ${x} and then puts in ${y} in repairs.  This increased the value of the house by {z}%.  How much profit did he make?

#variables:
- name = sample(names)
- x = range(0, 1000000)
- y = range(0, 1000000)
- z = range(0, 1000)
- ans = range(0, 10000)

#conditions:
- (x * z) % 100 == 0
- x * z / 100 >= y
- ans == x * z / 100 - y
