{city1} has {x} times as many {things} as {city2}. {city2} has {y} times as many {things} as {city3}. How many {things} do {city1}, {city2}, and {city3} have together if {city3} has {z} {things}?

#variables:
- city1 = sample(["Seattle", "Toulouse", "Charleston", "San Diego", "Detroit", "Hanover", "Toronto"])
- city2 = sample(["Seattle", "Toulouse", "Charleston", "San Diego", "Detroit", "Hanover", "Toronto"])
- city3 = sample(["Seattle", "Toulouse", "Charleston", "San Diego", "Detroit", "Hanover", "Toronto"])
- things = sample(["sheep", "horses", "monkeys", "ants", "trees", "people", "trains", "computers", "rivers", "bridges", "cars"])
- x = range(2, 100)
- y = range(2, 100)
- z = range(0, 100)
- ans = range(0, 10000000)

#conditions:
- city1 != city2
- city2 != city3
- city1 != city3
- ans == z + y * z + x * y * z
