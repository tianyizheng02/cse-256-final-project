In a dance class of {x} students, {y}% enrolled in {style1} dance, {z}% of the remaining enrolled in {style2} dance, and the rest enrolled in {style3} dance. What percentage of the entire students enrolled in {style3} dance?

#variables:
- x = range(0, 100)
- y = range(0, 100)
- z = range(0, 100)
- w = range(0, 100)
- v = range(0, 100)
- style1 = sample(["contemporary", "jazz", "hip-hop", "interpretive", "ballroom", "square", "line", "tap"])
- style2 = sample(["contemporary", "jazz", "hip-hop", "interpretive", "ballroom", "square", "line", "tap"])
- style3 = sample(["contemporary", "jazz", "hip-hop", "interpretive", "ballroom", "square", "line", "tap"])
- ans = range(0, 100)

#conditions:
- style1 != style2
- style2 != style3
- style1 != style3
- (x * y) % 100 == 0
- (x * (1 - y / 100) * z) % 100 == 0
- ans == (100 - y) * (1 - z / 100)
