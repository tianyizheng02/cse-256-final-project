A new program had {x} downloads in the first month. The number of downloads in the second month was {y} times as many as the downloads in the first month, but then reduced by {z}% in the third month. How many downloads did the program have total over the three months?

#variables:
- x = range(0, 100)
- y = range(0, 100)
- z = range(0, 100)
- ans = range(0, 10000)

#conditions:
- x * y * z / 100 == int(x * y * z / 100)
- ans = x + x * y + (x * y - x * y * z / 100)
